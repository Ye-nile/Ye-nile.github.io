Theoretical Condensed-Matter Physics

Jinyuan Ye 叶锦缘

I work on theoretical condensed-matter physics: strongly correlated electrons, where kinetic energy and Coulomb repulsion are of comparable size and the textbook pictures stop working. My question is whether magnetism can come from motion alone — the answer so far is a ferromagnetic insulator, published in Physical Review Letters.

我做理论凝聚态物理，研究强关联电子体系——在这里，动能与库仑排斥量级相当，教科书式的 图像会失效。我的问题是：磁性能否只由电子的运动产生？目前给出的答案，是 PRL 上那个 铁磁绝缘体。

About

关于我

I am a Ph.D. candidate in theoretical physics at Fudan University, in a joint programme with Westlake University, working with Prof. Congjun Wu. I expect to graduate in June 2027. My field is strongly correlated electron systems — the regime where kinetic energy and Coulomb repulsion are of comparable size, and neither the band picture nor a textbook ordered state is a good description.

我是复旦大学理论物理方向的博士研究生，与西湖大学联合培养，师从吴从军教授，预计 2027 年 6 月毕业。研究对象是强关联电子体系：在这个区间里，动能与库仑排斥量级相当，能带图像和 教科书式的有序态都不再是好描述。

I came to physics out of curiosity about how the world works, and stayed because I like thinking — and because I wanted to find out how far that gets me. Choosing theoretical physics for the Ph.D. was not a difficult decision: if the aim is to understand the rules underneath a phenomenon, the theory side is where the rules get written down.

我学物理，是出于对世界如何运转的好奇，也因为喜欢思考、想探一探自己智力的边界。 保研时选择理论物理几乎没有犹豫：如果目标是理解现象背后的规律，那规律是在理论这一侧 写下来的。

The question has a classic answer: Nagaoka showed that a single hole can polarise a half-filled band. But the theorem demands exactly one hole and U → ∞, and real materials satisfy neither condition. So we asked a narrower question — what is the smallest model that still produces ferromagnetic coupling, and how far can its conditions be relaxed? The trimer is that model. What we did not expect is that at 1/3 filling it is also a ferromagnetic insulator, with a direct competition between ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic order. That combination is rare: an insulator in practice is almost always antiferromagnetic.

这个问题有一个经典答案：Nagaoka 定理证明，半满能带中恰好一个空穴可以把整个体系极化。 但定理要求恰好一个空穴、且 U → ∞，现实材料几乎不同时满足。 于是我们退一步问：能产生铁磁耦合的最小模型是什么，它的适用条件能放宽到什么程度？ 三聚体就是这个最小模型。没有预料到的是，它在 1/3 填充时同时是一个铁磁绝缘体， 体系里存在铁磁与反铁磁的直接竞争。这种组合很少见——现实中的绝缘体几乎总是反铁磁的。

Our Physical Review Letters paper answers that question for a Hubbard model on a trimerized triangular lattice at 1/3 filling: a ferromagnetic insulator in which the magnetisation is driven by motion alone, without exchange or Hund's coupling. Each trimer becomes a spin-1 moment, and the inter-trimer hopping generates two competing superexchanges: one that never forms a double occupancy and scales with the intra-trimer hopping t (ferromagnetic), and one that costs U (antiferromagnetic). As U → ∞ only the ferromagnetic channel survives; as the interaction weakens, antiferromagnetic order takes over around U/t ≈ 13–15.

我们发表在 Physical Review Letters 的工作，在 1/3 填充的三聚体化三角晶格 Hubbard 模型上回答了这个问题：磁化完全由电子的运动驱动，不需要交换作用，也不需要洪特耦合。 每个三聚体成为一个自旋 1 局域矩，三聚体之间的跃迁产生两种 相互竞争的交换通道：一种不形成双占据、能量尺度由三聚体内部跃迁 t 决定（铁磁）， 另一种需付出 U（反铁磁）。U → ∞ 时只剩铁磁通道；相互作用减弱后， 反铁磁序在 U/t ≈ 13–15 附近占据主导。

What I actually enjoy is the moment a vague phenomenon starts to behave like a model. The first version is always crude — it keeps only the factors that matter most, and strictly speaking it is not true. Getting it to work is half the job; the interesting half is putting back what you left out, because that is usually where the next piece of physics is hiding. In the published work, the infinite-interaction limit gave a clean ferromagnetic insulator; restoring a finite U exposed the competition between ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic order that the clean limit cannot see. In the loop-current problem we deliberately started from a truncated set of hopping terms, skipping a nearer range that is only negligible when a symmetry forbids it. Putting it back shifted the numbers without touching the mechanism. Both outcomes were informative: one added a phase, the other removed a worry.

我真正享受的，是一个模糊的物理现象开始像模型一样运转的那个时刻。最初的版本总是粗糙的 ——只保留最重要的几个因素，严格说来并不符合事实。让它跑通只是一半，有意思的是另一半： 把省掉的东西加回去，因为下一块物理通常就藏在那里。在已发表的工作里，无限大相互作用 极限先给出了一个干净的铁磁绝缘体；把有限的 U 加回来后，才暴露出干净极限看不见的 铁磁—反铁磁竞争。环流序的问题上，我们刻意从一个截断的跃迁集合出发，跳过了更近的一档—— 只有在对称性明确禁止时，这一档才可以忽略。把后续项补回去之后，结果只是数值上移动，机制 并没有改变。两种结果都有信息量：一个多出了一个相，另一个去掉了一层担心。

A claim of that kind only stands if the numbers stand. During review I took over an existing tensor-network code base, ran DMRG on 6 × 4 × 3 clusters up to bond dimension χ = 9600, and analysed spin and charge correlation functions. When a referee questioned the metallic state, I designed the alternative analysis, introduced finite-entanglement scaling, and traced how the correlation length depends on the bond dimension — evidence for the metal, and a clear statement of where the phase boundary stops being trustworthy.

这类结论只有在数值站得住时才立得住。审稿阶段我接手一套既有的张量网络程序，在 6 × 4 × 3 体系上完成键维数至 χ = 9600 的 DMRG 计算，分析自旋与电荷关联函数。当金属态是否存在受到质疑时， 我设计了替代分析方案，引入有限纠缠标度，考察关联长度随键维数的变化——既为金属态提供证据， 也明确相边界估计在何处不再可信。

Since then I have spent more time designing methods than using them: a Gutzwiller variational Monte Carlo scheme in Julia, built module by module with AI assistance and reviewed line by line; and a DMRG study of loop-current order on the Kagome lattice. I care about the craft around the physics as much as the physics itself — code that can be read, interfaces that are tested, derivations that survive being written down.

最近我更常做的是设计方法而不是只用方法：用 Julia 逐模块搭建、逐行审查的 Gutzwiller 变分蒙特卡洛方案，以及 Kagome 晶格上环流序的 DMRG 研究。物理之外的手艺我也同样计较： 代码要读得懂，接口要测过，推导要经得起写下来。

Research

研究方向

Ferromagnets are supposed to need exchange. In a model we built, motion alone is enough — and what comes out is an insulator. 铁磁体按理需要交换作用。在我们搭的模型里，光靠电子的运动就够了—— 而且得到的还是一个绝缘体。

Kinetic magnetism & ferromagnetic insulators

动能驱动磁性

Ferromagnetism that comes from the kinetic energy of electrons rather than from exchange. Minimal models on frustrated lattices, the infinite-interaction limit, and how the mechanism survives at finite U.

铁磁序由电子的动能而非交换作用驱动：阻挫晶格上的最小模型、无限大相互作用极限， 以及这一机制在有限 U 下的存活条件。

Method design: Gutzwiller Monte Carlo

方法设计：变分蒙特卡洛

Gutzwiller variational wave functions with Monte Carlo sampling, aimed at single-particle excitation energies and their extrapolation — a quantitative route into the metal–insulator question.

以 Gutzwiller 变分波函数配合蒙特卡洛抽样，目标量是单电子激发能及其外推， 为金属—绝缘体判据提供定量依据。

Tensor-network methods

张量网络数值方法

DMRG and iMPS for frustrated two-dimensional systems: correlation functions and correlation lengths, and finite-entanglement scaling as a way to decide how far a numerical conclusion reaches.

用 DMRG / iMPS 研究阻挫二维体系，计算关联函数与关联长度；以有限纠缠标度判断 一个数值结论究竟能推到多远。

Loop-current order

环流序的形成机制

Ordered electron currents on the Kagome lattice. What role kinetic energy plays in stabilising the order, and which correlation functions would falsify the proposed mechanism.

Kagome 晶格上的有序环流：动能在这类序的形成中起什么作用，以及哪些关联函数 足以否证所提出的机制。

Both threads are still running: the metal–insulator question at finite doping, and loop-current order on the Kagome lattice. 两条线都在进行中：有限掺杂下的金属—绝缘体问题，以及 Kagome 晶格上的环流序。

Publications

论文

Physical Review Letters 136, 086504 (2026) First author Open access

Kinetic Energy Driven Ferromagnetic Insulator

动能驱动的铁磁绝缘体

Jinyuan Ye, Yuchi He, Congjun Wu

A minimal model of interacting fermions with a ferromagnetic insulating phase: the Hubbard model on a trimerized triangular lattice. At 1/3 filling each trimer forms a triplet spin-1 moment, and the inter-trimer superexchange is ferromagnetic, J = (2/27)·t′²/t, as U/t → ∞. At finite interaction the antiferromagnetic superexchange competes and the system enters a frustrated antiferromagnetic insulator around U/t ≈ 13–15, as confirmed by DMRG. The ferromagnetic state survives a weak staggered flux and becomes a ferromagnetic metal on light doping; the same analysis on the trimerized Kagome lattice yields only antiferromagnetic superexchange.

三聚体化三角晶格上的 Hubbard 模型：1/3 填充时每个三聚体成为自旋 1 三重态， 三聚体间交换在 U/t → ∞ 时呈铁磁；有限相互作用下 反铁磁通道与之竞争，在 U/t ≈ 13–15 附近进入阻挫反铁磁 绝缘体（经 DMRG 确认）。铁磁态在弱交错磁通下保持稳定，轻掺杂后转变为铁磁金属； 对三聚体化 Kagome 晶格作同样分析则只得到反铁磁交换。
